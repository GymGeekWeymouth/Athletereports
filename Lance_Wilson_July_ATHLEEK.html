What We Measured
ATHLEEKAnalyseMetric Glossary
What the numbers mean
A plain-language guide to every metric in this report
Jump Height
CMJ Height. How high off the ground you jumped from a countermovement start. One of the most well-normed metrics in the report. Affected by technique and body proportions, so best read alongside power and reactive strength rather than in isolation.
Reactive Strength
RSI Modified. Jump height divided by total movement time. Rewards both how high and how quickly you jump: a measure of spring and explosiveness. Sensitive to technique and a good marker of elastic, reactive quality.
Explosive Power
Peak Power (rel.). The highest power output during the jump per kilogram of bodyweight. Can be elevated by a deep countermovement; best interpreted alongside jump height and RSI together rather than in isolation.
Leg Symmetry
Limb Asymmetry (CMJ). The percentage difference in force between left and right leg during the jump. Under 10% is considered well-balanced. A well-normed and practically meaningful metric: one of the most reliable results in this report.
Maximum Force
IMTP Peak Force. The maximum force produced during a maximal pull against a fixed bar, relative to bodyweight. A measure of overall lower body strength with good literature support. Responds well to structured strength training and is a highly trainable quality.
Rate of Force Development
RFD @ 200ms (IMTP). How quickly force rises in the first 200 milliseconds of a maximal pull. Captures neuromuscular speed and explosiveness. Presented as a descriptive category rather than a ranked band in this report because it is highly sensitive to testing setup, sampling rate, and onset detection: it is most useful as a longitudinal tracking metric over time.
Postural Stability
Sway Velocity. Average speed of the body's centre of pressure while standing still. Lower is better. Presented as a descriptive category: most useful as a longitudinal baseline and for fatigue or return-to-play monitoring. Lower readings indicate steadier balance.
