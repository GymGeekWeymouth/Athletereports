How to read this

This document sets out Lloyd's money three ways. Everything comes back to these three ideas:

  • Brings in — money Lloyd earns directly from his own clients, gym sessions and the treatment room. This is his own effort.
  • Takes home — everything the business actually pays Lloyd: salary, dividends, cash and expense repayments. This is usually the bigger number, because it includes more than just his own clients.
  • The bills — the cost of running the business. Lloyd covers a share of these, and it comes off what he brings in.

The document works through each of these in turn, then the summary near the end brings them together for May, June and July.
